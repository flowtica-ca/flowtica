Capabilities

Intake & Validation

Turn messy inputs into structured work. We ingest requests from shared inboxes, forms, portals, and spreadsheets, then validate completeness, enforce required fields, and route unclear items into an exception queue.

Workflow Automation & Integration

End repetitive copy-paste. We connect your existing tools (accounting, CRM/helpdesk, scheduling, email) so statuses, assignments, and records stay in sync automatically.

Approvals, Exceptions & Controls

Happy path automated; exceptions handled by humans. We build approval routing, exception queues, retries, and guardrails so workflows stay safe, debuggable, and predictable.

Document Intelligence (with citations)

Answers grounded in your documentation. For policies, invoices, contracts, and SOPs, we use retrieval with transparent citations so humans can verify outputs before acting.

Client-Owned Deployments

Your data stays under your control. We deploy workflows in your environment (your cloud or controlled infrastructure) so you retain ownership, auditability, and compliance alignment.

Production Ops & Iteration

Keep it reliable after launch. Monitoring, alerting, logging, and iterative improvements tied to metrics (cycle time, touches, backlog, error rate) so automation doesn’t quietly degrade.
