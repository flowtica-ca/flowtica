Structured outreach work starts before any message is sent

Some teams do not need more outbound activity. They need a cleaner way to research accounts, collect public-source evidence, prepare drafts, and route that work through internal review. A CASL-first workflow is about putting research, evidence, and decision points in order before anyone uses the output for outreach.

What this workflow is for

  • Prospect research intake for founders, RevOps, or outbound operators
  • Public-source evidence capture tied to the account or contact record
  • Draft generation for internal review based on the captured context
  • Workflow routing so research, draft, and approval states are visible to the team

How evidence and review fit into the process

The workflow captures public-source notes alongside links or references so reviewers can see what information supported the draft. That keeps the process reviewable and gives your team a clear place to confirm whether a record should move forward, pause, or be rejected.

Research intake

  • Target list or manual prospect selection
  • Public-source research tasks
  • Evidence capture with references
→

Draft workflow

  • Context packaged for internal review
  • Draft generation based on captured notes
  • State tracking for revisions and approvals
→

Human review

  • Review of evidence, audience fit, and messaging
  • Explicit approval or rejection before any downstream use
  • Logged handoff into the next approved step in your process

What remains human-reviewed

  • Whether a prospect should move forward in the first place
  • The final message, positioning, and approval to contact
  • Any sending activity, channel choice, or compliance judgment
  • Exception handling when evidence is weak, incomplete, or contested

Process boundaries

This page describes workflow structure, not a legal guarantee or a promise about outreach results. The human team still owns approval rules, message decisions, and any downstream sending process.
