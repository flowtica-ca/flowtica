Accounts Payable Automation in 2 Weeks

Example · Finance Ops

Context

AP teams are often buried in invoice email threads, manual re-keying, and approval chasing across tools. The result is slow cycle time, inconsistent controls, and poor visibility into what is stuck and why.

What the MVP automates

  • Invoice intake from AP inbox and attachments
  • Field extraction and validation checks
  • Rule-based routing to the right approver
  • Approval and rejection capture with reason codes
  • Posting/sync to accounting with status tracking
  • Exception queue for low-confidence or policy-break cases

Architecture at a glance

End-to-end path: intake → validation → approvals → posting → updates.

Intake

  • AP inbox capture
  • Attachment parsing
  • Deduping by hash and invoice ID
→

Validation

  • Required-field checks
  • Vendor and amount validation
  • Exception flags for review
→

Approvals

  • Rules by threshold/cost center
  • Approver notifications
  • Timestamped decision logging
→

Posting + updates

  • Post/sync to accounting
  • Status updates and retries
  • Audit log for every step

How it behaves in practice (example invoice)

  1. A vendor emails Invoice #8843 for $12,420 due in 14 days.
  2. The workflow extracts fields, validates totals, and checks duplicates.
  3. Routing sends it to the correct approver based on threshold and cost center.
  4. The approver approves in one click; decision and timestamp are logged.
  5. The invoice is posted/synced and status is updated automatically.

What success looks like

  • Lower cycle time from invoice received to approved/postable
  • Fewer manual touches per invoice
  • Smaller exception backlog with clearer causes
  • Reliable audit trail for review and compliance

Typical delivery plan

  1. Map one AP workflow and approval matrix
  2. Configure intake, extraction, and validation on real invoices
  3. Implement routing, approvals, and posting/sync
  4. Launch with logs, runbook, and exception handling
  5. Review outcomes and scope next improvements
