Case Studies & Demos

Two repeatable MVPs we deploy for operations teams

Flowtica works best when workflows are treated like infrastructure: integrated, observable, and built for real day-to-day operations. Below are two focused demos aligned with our back-office automation sprint.

Example · Property Management

Dispatch and admin workflow automation

Automate maintenance intake, triage, vendor dispatch, and resident updates so requests stop living in inboxes and spreadsheets. This MVP connects to your current tools and adds dependable routing, ownership, and follow-through.

  • What it automates: intake from email/forms, urgency classification, dispatch routing, resident updates, status tracking, and escalations.
  • What changes: fewer manual handoffs, faster first response, and clearer ownership across dispatch and admin teams.
  • Typical outcomes: lower coordinator load, fewer escalations, and better visibility into aging tickets and bottlenecks.
