Maintenance coordination automation in 2 to 3 weeks

Example · Accounts Payable

Accounts payable automation in 2 weeks

Invoices arrive through email, portals, and PDFs—then someone re-keys data, chases approvals, and updates statuses. This MVP automates intake → validation → approvals → posting/sync, leaving only exceptions to humans.

  • What it automates: invoice intake, data extraction, duplicate checks, PO/GL validation, approval routing, posting to accounting, and vendor/internal updates.
  • What changes: fewer touches per invoice, faster cycle time, and a clean exception queue for edge cases.
  • Typical outcomes: reduced backlog, fewer late payments/fees, better audit trail and visibility.
