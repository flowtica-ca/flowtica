Accounts Payable and Back-Office Automation

Solution · Finance Operations

Where AP and repetitive back-office work slow down

AP teams are often dealing with the same pattern every week: invoices arrive from multiple channels, data gets re-entered, approvals are chased across inboxes, and exceptions sit until someone has time to investigate. The work is repetitive, but the exceptions are real, which is why the right fit is a controlled workflow with clear review boundaries rather than a black-box shortcut.

What gets automated

  • Invoice intake from AP inboxes, forwarded PDFs, and simple upload paths
  • Field extraction for vendor, invoice number, dates, totals, tax, due date, and related references
  • Approval routing by entity, threshold, department, cost center, or other internal rules
  • Exception queues for duplicates, missing data, mismatches, and posting failures
  • Status tracking so teams can see what is pending, blocked, approved, or ready for export
  • Audit-friendly logs for the workflow path and each human decision point

What changes operationally

  • Invoices stop depending on manual inbox triage before they enter the approval path
  • Approvers get a cleaner workflow instead of scattered follow-up threads
  • Finance has one place to review why an invoice is pending or blocked
  • Month-end and posting handoffs get less dependent on cleanup work and memory

Where exceptions stay with humans

This workflow is designed to reduce repetitive handling, not to hide judgment calls. Exceptions still stay with people when vendor matching is unclear, invoice data is incomplete, supporting context is missing, or the approval path needs a deliberate override.

Invoice intake

  • Inbox and attachment capture
  • Validation for missing or duplicate inputs
  • Structured field extraction

Routing and review

  • Approval rules by threshold and ownership
  • Exception queue for mismatches and uncertain cases
  • Reminder and escalation paths for stalled approvals
→

Posting handoff

  • Ready-for-export state after approval
  • Audit trail of status changes and decisions
  • Retryable path when a downstream sync fails

What success looks like

  • Fewer manual touches per invoice before it is approved or ready for posting
  • Better visibility into approval delays and exception causes
  • Cleaner auditability for who reviewed what and when
  • Less back-and-forth across email and chat for repeatable AP tasks
